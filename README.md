# 🐉 Rhiven's Skyrim SE Mods

**Skyrim Special Edition & Anniversary Edition — Modding Projects, Development and Technical Documentation**

Welcome to **Rhiven-SkyrimSE-Mods**, a repository dedicated to my personal modding projects for **The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition**.

This repository serves as a central workspace for developing, improving, modernizing and documenting Skyrim mods.

My goal is to create immersive, stable, configurable and maintainable modifications while sharing technical knowledge and development experiences with the Skyrim modding community.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

This repository focuses on several aspects of Skyrim modding:

- **Original Mod Development** — Creating new gameplay mechanics and immersive interactions.
- **Mod Modernization** — Revisiting older or discontinued mods when appropriate permissions are available.
- **Papyrus Scripting** — Developing, optimizing and troubleshooting gameplay scripts.
- **Creation Kit Development** — Working with quests, dialogue systems, AI packages, aliases and game mechanics.
- **Compatibility and Stability** — Improving interactions between mods and minimizing conflicts.
- **Technical Documentation** — Recording development methods, encountered issues, solutions and best practices.
- **Community Contributions** — Sharing improvements and technical findings with other modders.

## 🧩 Development Philosophy

My development approach follows a few important principles:

**Stability First**

Gameplay features should not compromise the stability of the game or interfere unnecessarily with other mods.

**Immersion and Player Choice**

Interactions should feel natural within Skyrim's world while allowing meaningful decisions and consequences.

**Modular Architecture**

Whenever practical, systems should be separated into reusable, maintainable components.

**Respect for Original Authors**

When improving or rebuilding existing mods, original authors and contributors must receive appropriate credit, and all applicable permissions must be respected.

**Documentation Matters**

Development decisions, technical challenges and solutions should be recorded to make future maintenance and community contributions easier.
